## Overview

This lab explored the ethical considerations of machine learning, including fairness, bias, transparency, and responsible AI development. I examined how machine learning models can unintentionally produce biased results and learned strategies for developing more ethical and trustworthy AI systems.

## Objectives

- Understand the importance of ethics in machine learning
- Identify different sources of bias in datasets and models
- Explore fairness and responsible AI principles
- Discuss the societal impact of machine learning systems

## Skills Practiced

- Machine Learning Ethics
- Responsible AI
- Bias Detection
- Fairness Analysis
- Critical Thinking
- AI Governance

## What I Learned

In this lab, I learned that building an accurate model is only one part of developing machine learning systems. It is equally important to consider fairness, transparency, accountability, and the potential impact of AI on individuals and society. This lab helped me recognize the importance of identifying and reducing bias while developing responsible machine learning solutions.
